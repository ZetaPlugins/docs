WYG lets players show off whatever they’re holding right in chat! Just type a simple placeholder in your message, and your item pops up for everyone to see. Others can hover over it to check out its name, enchantments, and all the details just like in your inventory.

## Features

- ✅ Show off main hand, off-hand and armor
- ✅ Use a simple command or placeholders in chat
- ✅ Quickly see what item a player is showing off with icons in the chat
- ✅ See all details of an item when hovering above the name in chat
- ✅ Fully configurable
