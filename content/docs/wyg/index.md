WYG (What'd Ya Got) lets players show off the item they're holding or wearing, right in chat. Type `[hand]` in a message and it turns into the item's name, with an icon. Other players can hover over it to see the enchantments, lore and every other detail, just like in an inventory.

```plaintext title="In chat"
Look what I just found: [hand]
```

## Features

- Share the item in your main hand, offhand or any armor slot
- Use a chat marker like `[hand]`, or a command like `/wyg hand`
- Send an item to one specific player instead of everyone
- Item icons in chat show at a glance what is being shared
- Full item details on hover
- Configurable format and hover text
